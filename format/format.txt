# Spanish Learning Notes

  ## Vocabulary
  | Spanish | English | Example Sentence |
  |---------|---------|-----------------|
  | hablar  | to talk | Yo hablo español. |
  | comer   | to eat  | Ella come pizza.  |

  ## Grammar Rules
  ### Present Tense (-AR verbs)
  | Pronoun | Ending | Example |
  |---------|--------|---------|
  | yo      | -o     | hablo   |
  | tú      | -as    | hablas  |
  | él/ella | -a     | habla   |

  ## Phrases
  > "¿Cómo estás?" — How are you?
  > "Muy bien, gracias." — Very well, thank you.

  ## Notes / Common Mistakes
  - **ser** vs **estar**: both mean "to be" but used differently
    - *ser* → permanent (Soy americano)
    - *estar* → temporary (Estoy cansado)
